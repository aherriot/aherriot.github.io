I am the Engineering Manager for the Web Experience team. Our mission is to accelerate organizational engineering velocity and ensure product consistency by building high-performance, React-based design system components, optimizing frontend infrastructure, and pioneering developer experience (DX) best practices. In addition to core infrastructure, my team delivers high-impact, organization-wide features that unify the user experience across all product suites.

## Projects

### Design System Modernization & Adoption

When I joined, SurveyMonkey's React-based design system was underutilized, with 90% of the codebase relying on legacy implementations. I led a company-wide modernization initiative that scaled system adoption from 10% to over 70%, drastically reducing engineering friction for hundreds of developers.

Beyond driving adoption, I championed the modernization of the underlying tech stack:

* Architecture Evolution: Led the 100% migration of our component library to TypeScript and orchestrated the replacement of a deprecated CSS-in-JS solution with a modern, high-performance CSS Modules architecture. This unlocked server-side rendering (SSR), optimized runtime performance, and enhanced styling flexibility.

* Quality & Process Engineering: Designed and built a custom visual regression testing framework and automated performance tests to guarantee component reliability.

* Community & Culture: Established robust open-source contribution workflows within the company, providing direct mentorship on accessibility (a11y), API architecture, and clean frontend design.

### Global Header & Navigation Consolidation

To solve a massive fragmentation problem—where 14 disconnected navigation implementations spanned various legacy and modern technology stacks—I designed a unified, tech-agnostic navigation architecture.

Originating from an award-winning hackathon project, I proved we could consolidate these systems into a single micro-frontend experience capable of seamless integration across the entire product ecosystem. This structural shift collapsed deployment timelines for global navigation changes from months to days, while instantly aligning the company on consistency and real-time notification capabilities.

### Enterprise Web Platform Migration

As a Senior Lead, I managed the modernization of our core B2B enterprise feature suite. We successfully transitioned a substantial portion of the enterprise product portfolio off legacy systems and onto a cutting-edge web platform powered by React and GraphQL. This initiative served as a catalyst for team growth, where I provided deep technical mentorship to upskill engineers on modern stack paradigms.

### GraphQL Architecture Scaling

I re-architected our federated GraphQL layer to break down data silos, designing a secure cross-user querying model. This architectural shift was a critical blocker for our enterprise product, enabling Admin accounts to securely audit and manage assets owned by team members. Leveraging this new data layer, I shipped high-value administration features, including seamless survey ownership transfers between team seats.

### Microservices

While primarily focused on frontend and UI infrastructure, I maintained a full-stack footprint by contributing to SurveyMonkey's backend ecosystem. Because our frontend organization fully owns and defines the GraphQL schema layer, I frequently bridged the gap to backend systems—directly developing secure REST and GraphQL APIs to expose and query our underlying Apache Solr search infrastructure.
